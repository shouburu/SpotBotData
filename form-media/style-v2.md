# Exercise form media standard · 2.0

Round 2 responds to the operator's reviews of the original ten examples. It compares three rendered looks across twenty exercises while retaining every earlier candidate and review. The original standard is preserved in `style-v1.md`. Canonical catalog instructions, equipment, laterality, and repetition counting remain authoritative.

## Three looks to compare

- **Porcelain studio**: a matte light-gray adult mannequin, charcoal fitted training kit, realistic proportions, restrained anatomical detail, soft studio light and a pale warm-gray background.
- **Warm ceramic**: the same ordinary adult proportions expressed as a warm sand-colored ceramic figure, muted teal kit, cream background and soft contact shadows.
- **Graphic 3D**: a solid, fully volumetric ivory figure in navy kit, crisp contour edges and restrained cel shading. This is an anatomical 3D figure, never a stick figure or skeleton schematic.

These are intentionally separate style candidates, not a claim that one identity has already been selected. Within each look, reuse a reference image for body proportions, clothing and material; save that reference's hash. A pose must be reviewed before its starting frame is animated. Each video must preserve its own starting frame's identity and apparatus.

## Framing and clarity

Make the body and relevant apparatus fill the image. Default to portrait 9:16 for upright movements. Use landscape 16:9 for floor-based or bench movements when portrait would shrink the body, hide an extended limb or lose a support contact. Keep only the margin needed for the complete movement path. Prefer a clear near-side or front three-quarter view with limited perspective distortion. Show the joints that teach the movement; do not obscure them behind equipment uprights or far limbs.

Do not apply the previous round's body markers to a new image. Round 2 has no body-attached guide overlays, unverified muscle maps or numeric joint angles. Show catalog muscle involvement in adjacent text. Any future overlay must be registered to its exact image and reviewed at phone size before use. Color can indicate named primary/supporting categories, never measured activation intensity.

Static posture examples receive clean alternate renders without markers. The floor-seated ankle pump and standing calf raise receive anatomical rendered demonstrations instead of the rejected SVG schematics. Outdoor Walking gets an optional rendered demonstration even though the text treatment was approved. Treadmill Walking gets a short posture demonstration as requested.

## Motion contract

Use a fixed camera, steady lighting, stable anatomy and physically plausible equipment. Demonstrate one controlled complete repetition in eight seconds by default, with brief readable start and end holds. Longer coordinated sequences may use ten seconds. No cuts, camera movement, generated labels, arrows, speech, music or reverse-played footage. Strip any generated audio from the delivery derivative.

For Dead Bug, use the operator's short as an observed movement reference: alternate opposite arm and leg, return to tabletop before changing diagonal, keep the trunk steady, and move the arm beyond the head rather than sideways. Avoid the source's clipped endpoints. Each diagonal extension-and-return is one repetition; a complete two-sided demonstration contains two repetitions. Repeated same-side movement, both arms moving together, lateral arm sweeps or pelvic rocking are grounds for rejection.

For Bench Press, show an already unracked bar, one complete controlled lower-and-press cycle, stable closed grip, supported head/upper back/hips and planted feet. Keep the chest endpoint visible. Rack setup and reracking remain in the Form text. The overhead still is a distinct angle, without guessed overlay coordinates.

Walking Lunge and Low Step-Up have different counting conventions in this catalog. Each completed forward lunge step counts once across both legs. A step-up ascent plus controlled descent counts once, with the entered target performed on each lead leg. Do not silently replace walking lunges with stationary lunges or floor-seated ankle pumps with chair-seated variants.

## External instruction

Prefer an attributed, movement-focused demonstration under one minute, ideally portrait. Verify actual duration and orientation when possible; record what was inspected. A YouTube Shorts URL alone does not prove a clip is under one minute. Link third-party clips rather than copying, cropping or rehosting them. Keep canonical Form text visible and explain any material mismatch with the demonstrated equipment or counting.

## Production and review

The cumulative ceiling remains **1,000 credits**, including the first round's 66 credits, references and rejected attempts. Verify the exact live offer before every submission. Reserve and record it first, generate one output at a time, then settle against the observed balance. Unknown prices, unexplained balance changes and uncertain submissions stop the queue. Reconcile before retrying. Three attempts are allowed per explicitly defined round/style/shot; a corrective retry retains the same shot identity. Opening the dashboard never generates media.

Round 2 is explicitly authorized additional production, including for approved first-round examples. It does not revoke those approvals. Keep the immutable first-round review snapshot, round identifiers, prompt and reference versions, actual costs, rejected outputs and current review history. AI editorial findings are separate from operator approval and later trainer review. Do not label a polished but incorrect motion as accepted.

Preserve masters outside Git. Deliver stills as WebP (maximum 500 KB), and silent H.264 720p MP4 with fast-start (target 1–2 MB). The selected package, including posters, must fit within 5 MB per exercise; unselected review alternatives are not shipped. Record actual dimensions, duration, bytes and hashes. Media descriptors remain local authoring data; no catalog schema or app hosting changes occur in this phase.

Review in the existing local dashboard at approximately 390 × 844 CSS px and a wider viewport. Preserve readable Form text, explicit playback, missing-media fallback and durable decisions. Automated tests, builds, lint, type checks, native checks and theme sweeps remain deferred.

## Delivery editing learned in Round 2

A generated master may contain a useful complete repetition followed by extra partial motion. Preserve that master and author a separate shorter delivery using a reviewed source interval. Keep the actual movement at its original speed, optionally holding its real start/end frames briefly. Never reverse movement, manufacture a return, or splice incompatible limb sequences. Delivery can be shorter than the eight-second generation default when the teaching point is complete. Record the source attempt and hash, trim interval, holds, measured output and zero generation cost; review the edited bytes independently. This produced compact complete-cycle ankle, press, row and pulldown options.

When correct pose pixels need more movement space, an authored canvas reference can preserve them and continue empty background edges. It is separately hash-tracked and reviewed before motion. Do not smear or extend an edge containing cropped anatomy or hardware; request a genuinely wider generated composition in that case. A visible inset-image boundary can become an unintended occlusion in generated motion.
