Pause overhead, lower the same bare bar under control onto the front shoulders, and hold that supported rack for the final second. This is one dip-drive, one receiving dip and one complete rack return. Both hands retain the same bar throughout; orange quadriceps stay visible during every phase and hold, other muscles gray.
