Position a flat bench under the Smith bar and set stops above head level so the bar cannot contact your face. Lie with feet planted and take a comfortable narrow overhand grip. Check a light, controlled range that fits the rail path before adding load.
Unhook the bar with arms extended. Bend the elbows to lower toward, but not onto, the forehead, allowing the upper arms to adjust enough to follow the rails without straining the elbows.
Extend the elbows to raise the bar without bouncing or turning the rep into a chest press. Rerack securely before sitting up; stop if the fixed path is uncomfortable.
