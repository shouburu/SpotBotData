Animate one cable lateral raise using only the far arm already extended beside the body. Keep its small elbow bend and lower this long arm slowly in a broad shoulder arc until the handle reaches across the front near the opposite thigh. Pause at the complete low endpoint. Lift the same long arm back out to shoulder height in its exact starting position and stop. Keep the other hand on the tower, torso steady and cable continuously attached to the same low pulley. Preserve the orange working shoulder. One eight-second high-to-low-to-high cycle, fixed camera, no elbow curl, partial pulse or added repetition.
