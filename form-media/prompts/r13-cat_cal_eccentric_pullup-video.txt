Canonical exercise: Eccentric Pull-Up
Place a stable box within comfortable foot reach of a pull-up bar, clear of the lowering path. Step up and grip overhand with the chin above the bar.
Take your weight through the arms, move the feet clear and lower slowly until the arms extend, without dropping or hitting the box.
Place the feet on the floor or nearby box before releasing the grip, then reset. Each controlled lowering counts once.
