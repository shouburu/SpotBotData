Canonical exercise: Strap-Supported High Step-Up
Secure a rated strap overhead inside a rack and place a stable box beneath it. Hold the strap for balance and place one foot fully on the box.
Push through that foot to rise onto the box, keeping the other foot free and using only the strap assistance needed for balance.
Bend the working leg to lower the free foot to the floor. Complete the target on each leg.

Counting and setup review notes: Whole working foot and floor-supported trailing foot are distinct in the start; enough overhead rise margin is mandatory. Future motion uses one actual working side and only balance assistance, not a hanging pull.
