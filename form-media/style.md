# Exercise form media standard · 10.0

Anatomical 3D is the approved default for new generated videos. Preserve previously accepted assets in their original styles and keep human decisions separate from AI editorial findings. Earlier standards and production experiments are recoverable from the Git checkpoint documented in [the repository guide](../README.md#history-and-local-cleanup).

## Appearance and framing

Use a silver-gray anatomical mannequin with readable muscle fibers, opaque charcoal shorts, a white background and restrained gray equipment. Show anatomically attached orange primary-muscle cues and subtler blue supporting cues. These illustrate muscle involvement, not measured activation intensity. Keep them visible through the working endpoint and return; reject misplaced, fading, floating or clothing-painted cues. A covered primary muscle needs a separately reviewed anatomical treatment before animation.

Use a fixed camera without cuts, generated text, logos, arrows, narration or music. Do not mention app sections or production context in the generation prompt. Frame the complete working envelope so joints, grips, equipment contacts and endpoints are readable on a phone. Prefer portrait when appropriate; floor/bench movements may need landscape. A closer supported view may omit irrelevant body parts but cannot hide necessary support or moving joints.

## Movement and source review

The catalog governs exercise identity, apparatus, instructions and repetition/per-side counting. Demonstrate exactly one complete repetition: readable start, full outward phase, turnaround, continuous full return and still finish. Do not ask for looping or add repeated-set/constant-tension cues. Preserve the original speed and never reverse footage to invent a return. Compound sequences need every required ordered placement. Do not alternate sides to fill time.

Inspect the exact hash-pinned starting frame before paid image-to-video generation. Pads, bench angles, cable routes, foot supports, grip orientation and equipment geometry must already be plausible. Write motion for the pose actually shown. A different starting phase is acceptable only with an honest setup phase and independently complete cycle. A reviewed source/reference does not guarantee correct generated movement.

Specify observable exercise-specific endpoints and which joints/equipment move or remain supported. Reject grip rotation, equipment morphing, extra partial cycles, incomplete range or substitution of another movement. Keep forearm support independent from repetition-count judgments, and verify that a lunge preserves the correct stepping foot. Do not impose constraints absent from the actual exercise. Shorter duration can reduce extra repetitions but cannot establish correctness; use fresh observed model/duration settings and review the output itself.

Review the delivered file across its whole timeline at dense intervals and inspect full-resolution endpoint frames. Sparse contact sheets can miss extra reversals or an incomplete final reset. Keep reference and motion findings tied to exact output hashes. AI candidates are a queue for human selection, never human acceptance or trainer certification. Quality holds, human rejections and provider failures are distinct states.

## Derivatives and retries

An original-speed contiguous trim is appropriate only when a complete correct cycle already exists. Disclose added still endpoint holds; they cannot replace motion. Preserve the source master, source interval, crop and exact derivative hashes, then independently inspect the delivered result. Do not trim away a necessary phase or hide a functional defect.

A color-only correction may remove a misplaced cue only with a reviewed mask that stays attached to the intended anatomy. Retain the source, mask/filter hashes and edit provenance, and inspect the entire derivative. Do not paint guessed anatomy. Construction diagrams may clarify source geometry but must not become the user's demonstration.

When a substitution repeats, change the physical source evidence or choose a suitable expert reference. Longer negative prompts or a higher-priced model do not establish quality. Any additional paid trial needs a distinct reviewed correction and the existing authorization/attempt guards; do not automatically repeat an expensive failure.

## Production controls

Use the guarded single-shot or explicit group runners, one output per isolated profile, fresh exact-model/mode/aspect/duration quotes, durable reservations and observed account settlement. Preserve lifetime spending and all historical attempts. The manifest owns the current period ceiling, round limits and explicitly authorized shot exceptions. Uncertain outcomes and unsettled groups block submission. Never reset history or infer a renewal from the date.
